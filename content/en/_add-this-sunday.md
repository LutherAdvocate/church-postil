#### The Angel’s Greeting: Hail Mary (Hail, Mary)



##### Hail, Mary, full of grace

> Hail, Mary, full of grace. The Lord is with you. Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb, Jesus Christ. Amen.

Here you see that there is no prayer contained within it, but only praise and glorification; just as there is no prayer in the rst words of the Lord’s Prayer, but rather praise and glorification of God for being our Father in Heaven. Therefore, we should neither oer a prayer nor an invocation of the Hail Mary, for it is not ting for us to interpret the words beyond what they say and how the Holy Spirit has placed them. However, they can be understood in two ways. First, as a narrative, in which we recount the grace that God has given her. Second, we may add to this a wish that she be recognized and honored by all.
